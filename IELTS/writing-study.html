Paraphrasing the question 🔁

Every essay starts here, and copied words do not count towards your total. This is free marks, and it takes about forty seconds once you have a method.

1

Change the nouns first

They are easiest. Teenagers becomes young people, cars becomes private vehicles.

2

Change the word class

The government spends becomes government spending. This changes more than a synonym does.

3

Reorder the clauses

Put the second half first. Structure counts as paraphrase, not just vocabulary.

Do not force a synonym for a technical word. If the table says social media, write social media. Inventing communal internet platforms is worse than repeating.

Writing an overview 🎯

The single highest-value paragraph in Task 1. Two sentences, no figures, straight after the introduction.

Not an overview
Television fell from 12 hours to 5 hours, while social media rose from 3 to 14.
An overview
Overall, the most striking change was the reversal between television and social media, while the remaining activities stayed broadly stable.

The difference is not length. The first one is data. The second one is a statement about the data as a whole — and it contains no numbers at all.

The two moves that make one

1

Name the biggest change

Which line moved most? Say that, without figures.

2

Name the contrast

What stayed the same, or moved the other way? One clause is enough.

Openers that signal an overview to the examiner: Overall, In general, It is clear that, The most striking feature is. Use one. They are looking for it.

The grammar that goes with them

PatternExample

The last two rows are the same fact written two ways. Using both in one essay is exactly what "flexibility" means in the band descriptor.

Taking a position ⚖️

Task 2 only. Without a clear stance in the introduction, Task Response is capped at 5 whatever else you do.

No position — capped at 5
This essay will discuss both sides of this issue and look at the advantages and disadvantages.
A position
While there are clear arguments on both sides, I believe the benefits of the policy outweigh its drawbacks, provided it is introduced gradually.

The second one tells the examiner what you think before they read a single reason. That is the whole requirement.

Frames that work for any question

Agree / disagree
I largely agree that…
While I accept X, I would argue that…
Discuss both views
Although both positions have merit, I find X more convincing.
Of the two views, I side with…
Advantages / disadvantages
On balance, the benefits outweigh…
The drawbacks are real, but in my view…
"On the fence" essays lose marks. If you genuinely see both sides, pick the one you can argue better and add a qualification later — that is what provided, as long as and although are for.

Developing one idea 🌱

A body paragraph with three ideas mentioned scores lower than one idea taken seriously.

1

Claim

The point of the paragraph, in one sentence.

2

Reason

because, since, this is largely due to. Why is the claim true?

3

Example

for instance, a good example is. Concrete, even if invented — examiners do not fact-check.

4

Consequence

as a result, which means that. Close the loop back to the question.

Test any paragraph you write: does it contain a because and a for example? If not, it is a claim, not an argument, and it will not score above 6.

Longer sentences 🧵

Only simple sentences caps Grammatical Range at 5, however accurate they are. You need variety, not length everywhere.

ToolUse it toExample
which / whoadd informationCities absorb heat, which keeps them warm at night.
althoughconcedeAlthough the policy is expensive, it works.
because / sincegive a reasonCosts rose because demand outpaced supply.
-ing clausejoin two actionsPrices fell, leaving producers with losses.
If / unlessconditionUnless funding increases, the scheme will fail.
Not only … butemphasiseNot only is it cheap, but it is quick.
Do not make every sentence long. A short sentence after two long ones is a deliberate effect and reads as control. Range means both.

Linking words ⛓️

The commonest way to lose Coherence marks is not too few linkers. It is too many, used mechanically.

Mechanical — band 6
Firstly, cars cause pollution. Moreover, they cause traffic. Furthermore, they are expensive. In addition, they are dangerous.
Natural — band 7
Cars pollute the air, and in dense cities they also bring the traffic that makes journeys slower than walking. The cost, both to owners and to the public purse, is a further problem.

The second version has one linker instead of four. Cohesion is mostly achieved by referring back — pronouns, repeated ideas, this, such — not by signposts.

A rough test: more than about one linker every two sentences and it starts to read like a list rather than an argument.

The last three minutes ⏳

Stop writing with three minutes left and do these four things in order. It is worth more than three extra sentences.

1

Count the parts of the question

Did you answer all of them? A missing part costs more than any grammar error.

2

Find your overview or your position

Point at it. If you cannot, add one sentence now — it is the cheapest band you will ever buy.

3

Scan for s endings

Third person verbs and plural nouns. It is the most frequent error type and the fastest to fix.

4

Check the word count once

Under the minimum is an automatic penalty. Know roughly how many words your handwriting puts on a line.

Do not reread for style. You will not improve the vocabulary in three minutes, and you may introduce a new error while trying.
