How to use these

  1. Do it once, properly. One take, timed, no restarting. A polished second attempt tells your teacher nothing.
  2. Try it before you reveal the model. On the study pages and the tests both. Reading a model without attempting it teaches you almost nothing.
  3. Send the result. Each page ends with what to send on WhatsApp. That is the part that makes the next lesson useful.
  4. Do not memorise anything. Examiners are trained to spot it and mark it down. Learn the shape, not the words.
