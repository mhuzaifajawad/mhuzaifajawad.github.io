If you are running out of time, sacrifice Task 1. It is worth half as much. Candidates who spend 35 minutes making Task 1 beautiful and then rush Task 2 lose more marks than they can possibly gain.

The four rules that decide your score

1. Do Task 2 first.

It carries twice the marks and needs the clearer head. Nothing says you must write them in order.

Do this: 40 minutes on Task 2, then 20 on Task 1. Set the clock and obey it.

2. Answer the question that was asked.

"Discuss both views and give your opinion" means both views and your opinion. Miss one part and Task Response is capped at 5 however good the English is.

Do this: underline the instruction words before you plan. Count the parts. Give each one a paragraph.

3. Under the word count is an automatic penalty.

And copying the question does not count towards it — markers discount lifted phrases.

Do this: paraphrase the question in your own words. The counter on this page subtracts copied text so you can see the real number.

4. One idea per paragraph, developed.

Two shallow ideas score lower than one idea with a reason and an example attached.

Test it: if a body paragraph contains no because and no for example, it is a claim, not an argument.

What each task is really asking

Task 1 — describe, never judge

You are reporting data. No opinions, no causes, no recommendations. The examiner wants selection: the main features, not every number.

The one thing: include an overview paragraph. A Task 1 answer without one is capped at 5 for Task Achievement no matter how good the English is. It is the commonest reason a competent writer scores 6.

Task 2 — take a position and hold it

An essay that sits on the fence, or states an opinion only in the conclusion, loses Task Response marks.

The one thing: say what you think in the introduction, then again in the conclusion. The marker should never wonder what your view is.

How Writing is banded

Four criteria, each scored, then averaged. Task 2 counts twice as much as Task 1 in your overall Writing band. This page can estimate two of the four from the structure of what you wrote; the other two need a human reader.

Between band 6 and band 7 the difference is rarely vocabulary. It is usually a missing overview, a missing position, or a paragraph that states an idea and never supports it — all three of which are checkable, which is what the next page does.

What to send on WhatsApp

  1. A photo or copy of the essay, handwritten or typed.
  2. Your structure score out of 10.
  3. How long it actually took you.
  4. The requirement you failed, and why you think you failed it.

Write it once and send it. Do not polish it for an hour — a polished essay tells your teacher nothing about what you can do in forty minutes.

Nothing is uploaded or saved. Refreshing clears your writing.
