Multimodal Human Activity Recognition 84.9% Detection F1

Built an end-to-end pipeline fusing RGB video and wearable IMU data to detect human transition activities in continuous streams. Designed a learned, input-conditioned gated fusion head that improved on maximum-score late fusion by +3.8% across 10 random seeds under strict leave-one-subject-out validation, winning on 9 of 10 seeds.

The learned gate produced stable, interpretable per-class trust weights — showing exactly when the model relies on motion versus visual evidence. Separately, tuning the temporal-suppression window delivered a further +13.8% improvement, unanimous across all validation folds.

Multi-View Activity Dataset Pipeline 81,681 frames

Transformed raw, unorganized multi-camera recordings into a fully model-ready dataset. Handled synchronization and quality control across four camera views, standardized viewport generation, and trimmed footage into 272 labelled activity clips.

Produced frame and subject-crop extraction at scale, multi-level annotation files (clip, temporal-boundary, step, sequence, frame, and crop level), CSV manifests, label maps, and subject-independent train/validation/test splits — plus baseline training notebooks for immediate experimentation.
