Core services

// 01 — VISION

Computer Vision Systems

Image and video models for classification, detection, segmentation, and activity recognition. Includes robustness testing for rain, low light, motion blur, and the other conditions that quietly break accuracy in deployment.

// 02 — SENSORS

Sensor & Time-Series ML

Models for wearable IMU, physiological, and multichannel sensor streams: signal preprocessing pipelines, CNN-LSTM and 1D-CNN architectures, and sliding-window detection with tuned thresholds.

// 03 — FUSION

Multimodal Fusion

Combining video, sensor, and other data sources into a single decision. Learned fusion heads that outperform naive averaging or max rules, while remaining interpretable enough to explain to stakeholders.

// 04 — VALIDATION

Evaluation & Interpretability

Rigorous validation designs (leave-one-subject-out, k-fold, subject-independent splits), Grad-CAM and integrated-gradients analysis, ablation studies, and clear reporting of what the model learned — including what it didn't.

// 05 — DATA

Data Pipelines & Annotation

End-to-end dataset construction: video trimming, frame extraction, subject cropping, multi-level annotation schemas, manifests and label maps, plus splits designed to actually test generalization.

// 06 — RESEARCH

Research Support

Literature review, experiment design, hyperparameter optimization with Optuna, reproducible notebooks, publication-quality figures, and LaTeX write-ups for papers or technical reports.

How I work

1. Scope — clarify the problem, the data you have, and what "working" actually means in numbers.
2. Baseline — get a simple, honest baseline running before anything clever. It sets the bar.
3. Iterate — improve the model with tracked experiments, not guesswork. You see the results as they land.
4. Validate — test on data the model has never touched, in the way that matches your real use case.
5. Hand over — documented, reproducible code plus a plain-language explanation of what it does and where it fails.
note: I report weak results as clearly as strong ones. If an approach isn't working, you'll hear it early — not after the budget is spent.
